Privacy policy

fitOS, a private household treadmill interface. Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Who uses it

Only the members of one household, on their own treadmill. There are no public accounts.

What data fitOS uses

  • Google / YouTube (scope youtube.readonly): the linked person's channel name, subscriptions, playlists and liked videos, to show them on the treadmill.
  • Spotify: the linked person's display name, playlists, Liked Songs, recently played tracks, current playback and queue; and, on request, playback commands (play, pause, skip, seek, volume, choosing a device).
  • Fitbit / Google Health (Google Health API scopes googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly and .writeonly, googlehealth.sleep.readonly, googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly): the linked person's daily sleep (duration and stages), resting heart rate, heart rate variability, SpO2, breathing rate and steps, and the times and types of their exercises (only to avoid logging the same run twice). If the person turns it on, each treadmill run is added to their Google Health record (type, start, duration, distance, calories, average heart rate). The daily values are stored on the household's server, attached to that person, to compute their recovery and strain on the treadmill. They are never shared, sold, used for advertising or used to train models, and are deleted when the person unlinks (or on request).

Where it is kept

Access is kept as OAuth refresh tokens on the household's own server at home, one per person who linked an account. They are never sent to the treadmill screen, never logged, and never shared with anyone. Content fetched from Google or Spotify is shown on the treadmill and kept only in short-lived memory caches. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Your choices

Unlink at any time on the treadmill (Settings → Accounts), which deletes the stored token. You can also remove access from your Google account or your Spotify account. Unlinking Fitbit also revokes fitOS's access at Google and deletes the health values that came from it.
