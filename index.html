fitOS

fitOS is a private, self-hosted interface for one household's treadmill. It runs on the treadmill's own screen and on a server at home. It is not a public service and has no sign-ups.

What it connects to

Each person in the household can choose to link their own accounts, from the treadmill's settings: YouTube (read-only: subscriptions, playlists and liked videos) and Spotify (playlists, Liked Songs, recently played, and playback control). Lyrics come from LRCLIB. Films and shows come from the household's own Plex server.

Linking happens on the person's own phone. Passwords are entered only on Google's or Spotify's own sign-in pages.
